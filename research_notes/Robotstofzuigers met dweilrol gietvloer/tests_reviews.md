# Independent tests and reviews of roller-mop robot vacuums (state as of September 2026)

Research date: 30 September 2026. Context: a household with 120 m² of smooth poured floor (gietvloer) in the kitchen and living areas, with chairs and rugs.

Important caveats for the report writer:
- Tweakers.net could not be reached; both search and fetch were blocked for this tool. There is **no Tweakers data** in these notes.
- RTINGS' "best robot vacuum" page did not render its content. RTINGS has reviewed the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow, but we could not read the review text. The only RTINGS result we could confirm is its top overall pick: the Saros 10R (spinning pads).
- Consumentenbond, Stiftung Warentest (test.de) and Which? put their per-model scores behind a paywall. We got only their methodology and partial or secondary information.
- **Vacuum Wars' scoring scale changed over time.** Older reviews compare dried-stain scores against an average of 110 and give "combined mopping" on a roughly 184-average scale. Newer reviews use averages of about 93–112 and a combined score around 20–23 or a "category score" around 2.7. Compare numbers only against the average quoted in the same review, not across reviews.
- Vacuum Wars dates below are the "last updated" dates the fetched pages showed. The original tests may be older. For example, the Aqua10 review mentions a December 2025 update.
- The model line-up has moved on. By September 2026 the Vacuum Wars top-20 includes newer roller-mop models: Ecovacs T90 Pro Omni and X12 OmniCyclone, Narwal Flow 2 and Freo 20, Eufy Omni S2 and C28, and Mova V70 Ultra. Several of the models named in the brief (X8, X9, Narwal Flow 1, Eufy S1 Pro) are now the previous generation.

## 1. What do lab tests say? (scores, rankings, measured results)

### Takeaway
Vacuum Wars is the only source we found that publishes standardised, comparable measurements for most roller-mop models. In its lab, the Dreame Aqua10 Ultra Roller, Ecovacs X11 OmniCyclone and Narwal Flow (1) measured well above average on dried stains. The Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow and Narwal Flow 2 measured well below average. The newest top performers are the Ecovacs T90 Pro Omni (roller) and the Eufy Omni S2 (roller, 24/24 obstacle avoidance). Consumentenbond, Stiftung Warentest and Which? results are paywalled, and the free data we found barely covers roller models.

### Inferences
- On measured dried-stain removal, the ranking of the models the user named is roughly: Ecovacs T90 Pro Omni (157, newer scale) > Dreame Aqua10 Ultra Roller ≈ Ecovacs X11 (136/110) > Narwal Flow 1 (125/110) >> Narwal Flow 2 (67.5/108) >> Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow (25/112). Being a roller mop is no guarantee of strong stain removal; how each brand implements it matters a great deal.
- Very low water residue (Curv 2 Flow 0.2 g, Flow 2 0.25 g) seems to go with weak stain removal. Vacuum Wars itself suggests this for the Curv 2 Flow. On a smooth gietvloer, low residue is good for streaks but may not dissolve sticky kitchen spills.
- For the Dutch buyer, Consumentenbond currently offers little guidance on roller mops, because Ecovacs and Narwal seem absent from its mopping comparison.

### Gaps
- There are no Tweakers reviews or Tweakers forum data (site blocked for this tool).
- Consumentenbond, Stiftung Warentest and Which? per-model scores are paywalled. It is unconfirmed whether any roller model (Ecovacs X-series, Narwal Flow, Dreame Aqua10 Roller) was graded by them.
- The RTINGS review content (for example the Qrevo Curv 2 Flow) was not retrievable.
- No independent lab numbers were found for the Eufy E28, Mova roller models, SwitchBot S20, Ecovacs X8 Pro Omni or X9 Pro Omni. The X9 has only user and aggregator data.
- No lab test specifically on "sticky kitchen spills" beyond anecdotal hands-on tests (Gizmodo BBQ sauce/mayo; X11 syrup and honey needing manual help).

## 2. Roller mops head-to-head vs each other and vs spinning pads (Saros 10R, Dreame X50)

### Takeaway
In the same Vacuum Wars lab era (average about 94–110), the best roller mops (Aqua10 Roller 136, X11 136, Flow 125, and the later T90 Pro Omni at 157) beat the flagship spinning-pad models: Saros 10R 103 and Dreame X50 Ultra 80, against an average of 94. The Saros Z70 (spinning pads) reached 139. The weak rollers (Curv 2 Flow, Flow 2) scored below average spinning-pad robots. Spinning-pad flagships remain better at obstacle avoidance (Saros 10R 24/24).

### Cited findings
- Roborock Saros 10R vs Dreame X50 Ultra (Vacuum Wars, updated 18 Sep 2026). Dried stain: Saros 103, X50 80, lab average 94. Water left: Saros 1.1 g, X50 0.5 g, average 1.0 g. Mopping category score: 3.6 and 3.5 vs 2.7. Carpet: 80% and 83% vs 75%. Obstacles: Saros **24/24** (Best Obstacle Avoidance mid-2025), X50 20/24, average 16.6. "The Saros excelled at stain removal, while the Dreame left floors noticeably drier, reducing streaking likelihood" — [Vacuum Wars Saros 10R vs X50](https://vacuumwars.com/roborock-saros-10-r-vs-dreame-x50-ultra-full-test-comparison/)
- The Roborock Saros Z70 (spinning pads plus robot arm) had the second-best dried-stain score at that time, 139 — [Vacuum Wars Saros 10R](https://vacuumwars.com/roborock-saros-10r-review/) (via search snippet)
- The Ecovacs X11's 136 dried-stain points were well above the Dreame L50 Ultra's 91 (spinning pads). The L50 left less water (0.35 g vs 0.7 g) and climbed higher thresholds (42 mm vs 23 mm) — [Vacuum Wars X11](https://vacuumwars.com/ecovacs-deebot-x11-omnicyclone-review/)
- The Curv 2 Flow's "dried stains" result is no better than traditional vibrating or rotating mops; its roller is "not the deep-stain weapon Roborock's marketing implies" — [bestrobovacuums](https://www.bestrobovacuums.com/reviews/roborock-qrevo-curv-2-flow-review) (search snippet); [Vacuum Wars Curv 2 Flow](https://vacuumwars.com/roborock-qrevo-curv-2-flow-review/)
- Roller vs roller corner cleaning: the Narwal Flow's extending wide roller fully cleaned 3 of 5 corner spots and mostly cleaned the other 2. The Dreame also did well thanks to a new corner algorithm — [The Smart Home Hookup mid-2025 comparison](https://www.thesmarthomehookup.com/mid-2025-ultimate-robot-vacuum-and-mop-comparison/) (search snippet)
- Rollers with an onboard dirty-water tank (Eufy S1 Pro) handle large wet spills without streaking. Pad mops saturate and streak — [Vacuum Wars S1 Pro](https://vacuumwars.com/eufy-s1-pro-omni-review-a-detailed-look-at-performance-and-value/) (search snippet)
- Edge weakness shared by many rollers: the Narwal Flow 2 "leaves a few-mm uncleaned strip at baseboards" (aggregator); the X11 leaves a thin strip along walls; the Curv 2 Flow is inconsistent at edges and corners — [SmartHomeExplorer](https://www.smarthomeexplorer.com/guides/best-roller-mop-robot-vacuums-2026); [T3 X11](https://www.t3.com/home-living/vacuum-cleaners/ecovacs-deebot-x11-omnicyclone); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/roborock-qrevo-curv-2-flow-review-ultimate-robot-mop-if-you-dont-have-corners-or-walls-2000745723)

### Inferences
- The best rollers (Ecovacs X11/T90, Dreame Aqua10 Roller, Narwal Flow 1) beat the Saros 10R and X50 on stain removal by about 30–60% in comparable Vacuum Wars runs. The Saros 10R wins clearly on obstacle avoidance, which matters for chair legs.
- On a large open gietvloer with few corners, the rollers' edge weakness matters less than stain performance and residue.

### Gaps
- No Consumentenbond, Test.de or RTINGS head-to-head of rollers vs pads was found.
- There is no standardised test of streaking on a seamless, glossy poured floor (gietvloer, PU or epoxy). All water-residue figures are grams left behind, not visual streaks.

## 3. Best and most reliable models, and known problems

### Takeaway
Among the requested models, the Ecovacs X11 OmniCyclone and Dreame Aqua10 Ultra Roller have the strongest measured mopping. The Narwal Flow (1) combines strong mopping with the best user-reported odour and clog behaviour. Each has known problems: Ecovacs first-run navigation and app, Dreame dock leaks and docking, and Narwal software quirks and pump priming. The Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow is a strong vacuum but a weak mop. Newer models (Ecovacs T90 Pro Omni, Eufy Omni S2) now lead Vacuum Wars on mopping plus obstacle avoidance.

### Cited findings
**Ecovacs X11 OmniCyclone**
- Poor first-run navigation needing several remaps: initial mapping took 30 minutes longer than average, and the robot "spent 17 minutes spinning in circles due to rug confusion". The internal LiDAR is worse than top-mounted units. The bagless system collects damp debris and needs manual cleaning. Small 220 mm (sic) dustbin. Modest 23 mm threshold climbing — [Vacuum Wars X11](https://vacuumwars.com/ecovacs-deebot-x11-omnicyclone-review/)
- T3 headline: "strong performance but AI features struggle to deliver" — [T3](https://www.t3.com/home-living/vacuum-cleaners/ecovacs-deebot-x11-omnicyclone)

**Ecovacs X9 Pro Omni (Reddit, aggregated)**
- Owners praise the extending roller for reaching walls and strong carpet suction. They report poor obstacle avoidance ("frequently gets stuck on small items and carpet edges"), an awkward app, lots of time spent docking, frequent water refills, and "unresponsive and unhelpful" customer support — [RedditRecs X9 Pro Omni](https://redditrecs.com/robot-vacuum/model/ecovacs-deebot-x9-pro-omni-robot-vacuum-and-mop/)

### Inferences
- Odour in the roller or station is the most common roller-specific complaint. It is less of a problem where the dock washes with hot water (Ecovacs X-series, Dreame 100°C, Mova 100°C) or self-cleans the dirty tank (Narwal Flow).
- For chairs and rugs, obstacle-avoidance scores and rug handling are the differentiators. Measured obstacle scores: Saros 10R 24/24, Eufy Omni S2 24/24, T90 Pro Omni 23/24, Aqua10 21/24, X11 and Flow 2 20/24, Flow 18/24, Curv 2 Flow 16/24. Separately, the X11 showed specific rug confusion on its first run.

## 4. User sentiment (Tweakers forum, Reddit, Coolblue), app quality, NL customer service

### Takeaway
Reddit sentiment (via RedditRecs) favours the Narwal Flow for mopping and low-odour maintenance. We found no Tweakers or Coolblue data and no Netherlands-specific service information.
