# Robotstofzuigers met dweilrol (roller mop): modellen, specificaties en NL-prijzen (stand september 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-30. Prices are as they appeared in search results or fetched pages on that date, unless noted otherwise. Prices move almost weekly, so check them again before buying. Budget: 21 tool calls. Several manufacturer and Coolblue pages returned only navigation, not spec tables (see Gaps).

## Q1. Which roller-mop models exist in 2025–2026 (NL/EU), including 2026 launches?

### Takeaway
By 2026 almost every major brand sells at least one roller-mop robot. Current NL/EU-relevant models: Ecovacs (X9 Pro Omni, X11 Pro Omni / X11 OmniCyclone, T90 Pro Omni, and the new T90S Pro Omni from IFA Sept 2026), Dreame (Aqua10 Ultra Roller Complete), Roborock (Qrevo Curv 2 Flow, its first roller), Narwal (Flow and Flow 2), Eufy (Omni S1 Pro, Omni S2, E25/E28, C28), Mova (Z60 Ultra Roller), SwitchBot (S20), Dyson (Spot+Scrub Ai), and budget/other brands (Yeedi M14 Plus/M16 Infinity, Lefant M210 Pro Omni, iRobot Roomba 705X). Dreame Matrix10 and most X50/X60 variants use spinning pads, not rollers (see Gaps).

- Older or superseded models: the Ecovacs X9 Pro Omni is the oldest (2024) and weakest on suction (16,600 Pa), and the original Narwal Flow has been replaced by the Flow 2. Both are still sold, but they are now "previous generation".
- In mid-range, the T90 Pro Omni (now about €529) is likely to be pushed out by the T90S Pro Omni once the T90S reaches the EU.

## Q2. Specifications per model

### Takeaway
For a 120 m² gietvloer (poured floor) with chairs and rugs, these are the specs that matter most: mop lift and carpet handling (10–15 mm lift on most models, 28 mm on Eufy S2), in-run roller rinsing (all of them, to different degrees), obstacle avoidance (all combine structured light with RGB camera and LiDAR), and robot height (95 mm on the T90 Pro Omni, 97.5 mm on the Dreame Aqua10, 119 mm on the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow). Every model covers 120 m² on one charge.

### Inferences
- **For a gietvloer with rugs:** mop lift is 10–15 mm on most models, 14 mm plus AutoSeal cover on the Dreame, and 28 mm on the Eufy S2. With thicker rugs, a model that covers or raises the roller high (Eufy S2, Dreame AutoSeal) is less likely to wet the rug. None of the sources confirms that the roller can be taken off and left in the station for vacuum-only runs.
- **Chairs:** all flagships combine structured light with an RGB camera. Narwal Flow 2 uses dual cameras with a VLM. Chair legs are handled by standard AI obstacle avoidance. The only independent obstacle score found is Narwal Flow 2 at 20/24.
- **Under furniture:** Ecovacs T90 Pro Omni (95 mm) and Dreame Aqua10 (97.5 mm) are the lowest. The Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow (119 mm) is noticeably taller.
- **Area:** every model easily covers 120 m² on one charge (Ecovacs PowerBoost handles up to 500 m² per task).

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether the roller can be detached or left in the station on any model. Manufacturer pages did not load their spec tables.
- No verified water hook-up kits for NL. The T90 Pro Omni has none according to Ecovacs US. Dreame often sells a separate "water refill and drain kit", but this was not confirmed for the Aqua10 Roller.
- Missing specs: Dreame Aqua10 roller rpm and pressure; Roborock tank sizes and noise; Narwal Flow 2 tank sizes and height; Eufy S2 height and tanks; details for SwitchBot S20 and Dyson Spot+Scrub Ai. The Dreame NL, Dreame global and Coolblue pages returned only navigation.

## Q4. Practical difference between roller-mop designs (how the mop stays clean during cleaning)

### Takeaway
Every roller system follows the same principle: fresh water is sprayed onto one side of the rotating cylinder or track while a scraper removes dirty water on the other side, so only rinsed mop surface touches the floor. The designs differ in pressure (12–15 N), rpm (170–240), number of nozzles, water temperature during cleaning (Narwal Flow 2 heats to 60 °C), and whether rinsing is always on or mainly done in the dock.

### Cited Findings
- General principle: the roller "sprays uncontaminated water on one side while a scraper squeegees accumulated grime off the other, so only fresh water contacts the floor". Flat spinning pads "drag one saturated cloth across the entire floor, accumulating grime until a dock trip" — [SmartHomeExplorer](https://www.smarthomeexplorer.com/guides/best-roller-mop-robot-vacuums-2026)
- Rinse frequency varies: eufy Omni S2 has "always-on in-run rinsing", while others do their main cleaning at the dock between runs — [SmartHomeExplorer](https://www.smarthomeexplorer.com/guides/best-roller-mop-robot-vacuums-2026)
- **Ecovacs OZMO Roller 3.0 (T90 series):** nylon cylinder at 200 rpm, rinsed by 16 (or 32) pressurised nozzles, dual-layer scraper removes dirty water, and the roller can extend sideways for edges (TruEdge) — [Ecovacs US](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/shop/deebot-robotic-vacuum-cleaner/deebot-t90-pro-omni); [Notebookcheck T90S](https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-robot-vacuum-with-roller-mop-offers-flagship-features-at-a-mid-range-price.1407651.0.html)
- **Narwal FlowWash (Flow / Flow 2):** a rolling track (belt) rather than a cylinder, with 12 N pressure and 60 °C water during cleaning. Dirty water is collected in a built-in, non-removable tank in the robot with an agitator — [Vacuum Wars](https://vacuumwars.com/narwal-flow-2-ultra-first-look/)
- **Roborock SpiraFlow:** 270 mm roller, 220 rpm, 8 water supply points, built-in scraper, 15 N — [Roborock NL](https://nl.roborock.com/store/products/roborock-qrevo-curv-2-flow-robotstofzuiger-spiraflow-realtime-zelfreinigende-rolmop/buynow)
- **Dreame Aqua10 Roller:** extending roller with a counter-roller scraper. A cover closes over the roller on carpet (AutoSeal) — [SmartHomeExplorer](https://www.smarthomeexplorer.com/guides/best-roller-mop-robot-vacuums-2026); [Notebookcheck review](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Cover-closed-mop-roller-gone-Dreame-Aqua10-Ultra-Roller-review.1120674.0.html)
- **Eufy HydroJet / HydroStream:** multi-nozzle spray. E28 has a dual scraper that rinses 360×/min. S2 runs at 240 rpm and 15 N — [SmartHomeExplorer](https://www.smarthomeexplorer.com/guides/best-roller-mop-robot-vacuums-2026); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/robot-vacuums/eufy-omni-s2-review)
- A roller picks up dirt "in één doorgang in plaats van uit te smeren" (in one pass instead of smearing it), which gives a cleaner result on stuck-on dirt — [uwproductwijzer.nl / reoverview via search](https://reoverview.nl/robotstofzuiger-met-dweilfunctie/)
- Test results do not always match the marketing: Narwal Flow 2 scored below average on dried stains (67.5 vs 108) before a firmware update ([Vacuum Wars](https://vacuumwars.com/narwal-flow-2-ultra-first-look/)). Techfi.nl found the Roborock "beter in zuigen dan in dweilen" (better at vacuuming than mopping) ([Techfi.nl](https://techfi.nl/huishoudelijk/roborock-qrevo-curv-2-flow-review)).

### Inferences
- Poured floors (gietvloer) show streaks and water marks easily. A roller that leaves little water behind helps; Narwal Flow 2 leaves 0.25 g residual water, well below average ([Vacuum Wars](https://vacuumwars.com/narwal-flow-2-ultra-first-look/)). This is an inference: no source tested specifically on gietvloer.
- The Ecovacs cylinder and the Narwal track have a similar goal. The track has a larger contact surface and heats the water while cleaning. The Ecovacs roller relies on pressurised rinsing and a scraper with unheated water during the run (only the dock uses hot water). No independent comparison test of the two was found.

### Gaps
- No independent head-to-head test of roller designs on streaks or residue on a poured floor (gietvloer).
- Nothing found on long-term wear or odour of the roller or track, or on replacement roller prices in NL.
